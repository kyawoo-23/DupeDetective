Looking at the code:
- `'planned'` → shows "Mark in progress" button → transitions to `'in-progress'`
- `'in-progress'` → shows "Mark complete" button → transitions to `'complete'`
- `'complete'` → shows confirmation text, adds to guidelines

The `'in-progress'` state is the middle step, but functionally it's redundant: for the guidelines, the only meaningful distinction is `complete` vs not complete. The backlog filter `migrationStatus !== 'complete'` treats `planned` and `in-progress` identically. **The simplification:** Remove `'in-progress'` — collapse it into `'pending'` (rename `'planned'` → `'pending'`). The two-state system `'pending' | 'complete'` covers everything distinctly: either the merge hasn't happened, or it has.
